# Third-party provenance

The source-faithful FreeFlux compatibility extraction in `validation/freeflux_runtime/` is derived from FreeFlux 0.3.8 and is covered by the upstream GPLv3 terms recorded in that directory. The Synechocystis validation package includes the same compatibility runtime for reproduction of the published benchmark.

Published benchmark data and model definitions retain their original scholarly provenance; the manuscript bibliography and the validation reports identify the corresponding publications and repository commits.
